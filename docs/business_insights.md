# Business Insights – KSGA Mart Malaysia (Jan–Jun 2026)

Activity 10: five insights drawn from the visualizations.

## 1. Sales peaked in June and grew every month except March
June recorded the highest sales at **RM 180,000**, a **50% increase** over January (RM 120,000). The only dip was March (RM 128,000, down from RM 135,000 in February); from April onward growth was steady.

## 2. Central region generates the most revenue
Central contributed **RM 180,000 (30.5%)** of regional sales, well ahead of Southern (20.3%) and East Malaysia (18.6%). **East Coast is the weakest at RM 85,000 (14.4%)**.

## 3. Profit is improving, and faster than sales
Profit rose from RM 35,000 to RM 78,000 (**+123%**) while expenses grew only **20%** (RM 85,000 to RM 102,000). Profit margin climbed from **29.2% in January to 43.3% in June**. June also had the largest sales-minus-expenses gap (RM 78,000), which equals the monthly profit.

## 4. Customer growth is increasing overall, but momentum is slowing
Customers grew from 1,200 to 1,700 (**+41.7%**). **April** had the highest growth (+200 customers, +16.0%), after a drop in March (-70). Monthly growth then eased to +130 in May and +120 in June. Average sales per customer also edged up from RM 100 to RM 106.

## 5. Recommendations to management
- **Revive East Coast and Northern:** run targeted promotions and review stock and store coverage in the two lowest-performing regions.
- **Replicate Central's playbook** (assortment, pricing, marketing) in other regions.
- **Protect the margin:** keep expense growth below sales growth as volume scales.
- **Re-accelerate customer acquisition:** loyalty and referral campaigns to counter the slowing monthly additions, and investigate the March dip for a seasonal or operational cause.

## Data note
The regional figures total **RM 590,000**, while monthly sales total **RM 878,000**. The brief does not explain the RM 288,000 difference (for example, unallocated or online sales). Before sharing regional conclusions with management, confirm what the regional dataset covers.
